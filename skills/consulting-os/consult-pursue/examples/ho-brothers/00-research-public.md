# Public research — Ho Brothers CustomHub agentic workflow

**Date:** 2026-05-22  
**Source:** Public website, press, LinkedIn. No client brief on file.

## Current state (Ho Brothers)

- **Business:** Chicago-based custom jewelry manufacturer serving **400+ independent retailers** nationwide; **100,000+ CAD designs** produced; SEC approach (Systems, Ease, Consistency). ([Capabilities](https://hobrothers.com/capabilities), [History](https://hobrothers.com/historyxyz))
- **CustomHub™:** Proprietary cloud platform — job management, integrated communication, real-time tracking, 3D render viewing, BI data; **white-label** option for retailers. ([Home](https://hobrothers.com/home), [National Jeweler feature](https://nationaljeweler.com/articles/12610-the-scalable-professional-and-effortless-solution-for-high-demand-custom-jewelry-retailers))
- **Process SLAs (public):** CAD render + pricing in **24–48 hours**; optional wax in **48 hours**; rush production **7 business days**, standard **15 business days**. ([Home](https://hobrothers.com/home))
- **Manufacturing stack:** Matrix/Rhino, Z-Brush, JewelCAD; 3D scanning/reverse engineering; DWS/Projet 3D printing; controlled-atmosphere casting; Sarine gemstone scanner. ([Capabilities](https://hobrothers.com/capabilities))
- **Scale signals:** Hong Kong entity; **Mumbai office operational Jan 2026**; presence in India and Mexico per LinkedIn. ([LinkedIn — Ho Brothers Custom Solutions](https://linkedin.com/company/hobrothers))
- **Differentiation vs. trade shops:** Standardized QC, dedicated account managers, risk-free first-job guarantee — positioning as **systems-led manufacturer**, not bench-only shop. ([National Jeweler](https://nationaljeweler.com/articles/12610-the-scalable-professional-and-effortless-solution-for-high-demand-custom-jewelry-retailers))

## Market / competitive signals

- **AI jewelry design platforms** (Diatech Studio, Aurevia, Tashvi, BLNG, Shamla Tech) push sketch-to-CAD, visual search over die libraries, and in-store customization — retailers may expect **AI-native intake and self-service**. ([Diatech Studio](https://studio.diatech.ai/), [Shamla Tech AI for Jewellery](https://shamlatech.com/ai-for-jewellery/))
- Ho Brothers already owns **production-grade CAD + CustomHub** — gap is not greenfield design AI but **agentic workflow** on top of existing portal (intake, revisions, exceptions, retailer co-pilot).

## Gap / opportunity (inferred)

CustomHub streamlines tracking and communication; next phase is **agentic job orchestration** — turn unstructured retailer requests into structured job briefs, automate revision loops against CAD approvals, proactively surface production exceptions, and optional white-label retailer assistant — **without replacing CustomHub or the CAD/production stack**.
