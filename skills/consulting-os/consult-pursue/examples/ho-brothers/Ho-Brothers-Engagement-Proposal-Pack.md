## Executive summary — primary decision

**Primary decision:** Whether to extend **CustomHub** from job tracking, 3D viewing, and account-manager coordination into **agentic end-to-end custom-job workflows** on 2–3 high-volume paths — structured intake, revision-loop automation, proactive production exceptions, and optional white-label retailer co-pilot — **without replacing CustomHub or your CAD/production stack**.

**Recommendation:** **Option B — 8-week CustomHub Agentic Workflow POC** on **retailer intake → structured job brief** and **status / exception handling** (rush delays, approval bottlenecks, stone/material flags).

**Why now:** Ho Brothers serves **400+ retailers** with a proprietary portal while AI-native jewelry platforms (design search, sketch-to-CAD, in-store customization) raise retailer expectations; global scale-up (e.g. Mumbai 2026) increases pressure on account-manager throughput — agentic layer de-risks scaling **on top of** CustomHub, not a rip-and-replace.

## Frame

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Definition of good** | ≥60% of pilot intake messages auto-structured into CustomHub-ready job briefs (human review queue); ≥50% reduction in account-manager touches on status/ETA queries in sandbox; eval rubric signed by ops + product; audit log for every agent action |
| **In scope** | Sidecar orchestration integrated with CustomHub APIs/events; 2 workflow paths; RAG over approved SOPs/pricing rules; human-in-loop on quotes and design approvals; retailer-facing status co-pilot (optional white-label slice) |
| **Out of scope** | Replacing CustomHub; greenfield AI CAD generation (Matrix/Rhino pipeline stays); ERP/MRP replacement; full production scheduling optimization |
| **Assumptions** | Sandbox/staging CustomHub access; English primary; account managers remain escalation path; IP/design data stays in Ho Brothers environment |
