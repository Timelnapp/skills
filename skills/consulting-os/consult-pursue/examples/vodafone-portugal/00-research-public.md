# Public research — Vodafone Portugal customer support AI

**Date:** 2026-05-22  
**Source:** Public Vodafone announcements + academic (VodaChat, IST Lisbon). No client brief on file.

## Current state (Vodafone Group / Portugal)

- **SuperTOBi** live in Portugal (Jul 2024): GenAI assistant on Microsoft Azure OpenAI; successor to **TOBi** (~45M questions/month, 13 markets).
- **Portugal results (appointment booking):** first-time resolution **15% → 60%**; online NPS **+14 pts to 64** ([Vodafone newsroom, Jul 2024](https://www.vodafone.com/news/newsroom/technology/meet-super-tobi-vodafone-s-new-generative-ai-virtual-assistant-now-serving-customers-in-multiple-countries)).
- **Billing journeys** being added; handoff to human when agent cannot answer.
- **SuperAgent** (agent assist) and **SuperSearch** complement customer-facing bot; 10-year Microsoft partnership; €140M CX investment reallocation (FY cited in release).
- **Group direction (2026):** agentic AI with Google Cloud (Business AI Concierge) in DE/GR — signals appetite beyond Q&A bots ([Google Cloud press, Apr 2026](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-04-22-Vodafone-Business-Launch-New-AI-and-Cybersecurity-Solutions-to-Accelerate-Small-Business-Digital-Transformation-in-Partnership-with-Google-Cloud)).

## Local research signal

- **VodaChat** (IST Lisbon / Vodafone): RAG over customer feedback for employee insight — hybrid BM25 + FAISS; Gemini 1.5 Flash vs Llama 3.1 evaluated; in use by Vodafone employees ([Técnico Lisboa record](https://scholar.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/records/ke293kLiTaBaOIDbIvS5HeWoe-63_MP6-i6j)).

## Gap / opportunity (inferred)

SuperTOBi improved **understanding and FTR on chat**; next wave is **agentic resolution** — multi-step journeys with BSS/OSS tool use, voice parity, eval harness, and alignment with SuperAgent — without replacing the Microsoft stack.
